Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Pipeline Studio ("we", "our", or "the Application") handles user information and data when integrating with third-party platforms like TikTok.

1. Information We Collect

Pipeline Studio is a personal creation suite. When connecting your TikTok account via OAuth 2.0, we may access the following authorized scopes:

  • Basic Profile Information (user.info.basic): We access your TikTok open ID, display name, and avatar solely to show connection status inside the publisher UI.
  • Video Publishing & Upload (video.publish, video.upload): We use these permissions exclusively to transmit video files, captions, and privacy options you choose to publish or draft to your TikTok account.

2. How We Use and Store Data

  • Local Storage: Authorization tokens (access tokens and refresh tokens) and account identifiers are stored securely on your local computer or private server environment.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, rent, or share your account data or tokens with any third parties or advertising networks.
  • Direct Transmission: Video files and publishing requests are transmitted directly between your local instance of Pipeline Studio and the official TikTok open APIs.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

You can revoke access and delete all stored tokens at any time by clicking "Disconnect" inside the publisher interface of Pipeline Studio, or by revoking application permissions inside your TikTok account settings. Upon disconnection, stored access and refresh tokens are permanently purged.

4. Third-Party Platform Policies

Our integration complies with the TikTok Developer Terms of Service and API Policies. For details on how TikTok processes your personal information, please refer to the TikTok Privacy Policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or requests regarding your data and privacy within Pipeline Studio, please contact the application administrator.
